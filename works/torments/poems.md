**Impossibility**

Sitting across the table 
People around us?
Conversation?

Our eyes meet - green.
I longed for this moment
I longed for you to look at me
For you to look *in* me
For how your pupils dilate a bit when our eyes connect
A fraction too long? 

I break the lock.
I speak loudly.
I make a joke. 
People seem to laugh 
You laugh too. Or do you? 

I have not been caught.
This time.

Is it my imagination? 
Conjuring an attraction, 
Presuming you feel the same,
Longing you feel the same,
Or at least a bit.
Yearning for us to be together alone.
But what would we say 
to each other then? 

We both know
Our love is forbidden 
By barriers we can’t hope to surpass -
We can’t hope - 
Without drifting apart. 

I laugh again and long 
for the next time our eyes collide.

**Hurt**

I hurt you because I want to feel
When silence means death
Pain is the last resort. 

What happened to our promises?
Our locked eyes, 
Our dreams once thought eternal?

A prison - 
a cage of expectations,
of friends, 
of a life built together, 
of commitments once felt hopeful?

I wish, oh, I so wish,
to rewire my mind.
For reason to prevail,
feelings to quiet,
geometry to overcome.
I wish 
empty smiles were enough. 

I keep hurting you 
Hoping for a miracle 
Until the cage collapses. 

--- 

**Holding Hands**

Holding hands
Not even pretending to be there
Silence, no conversation. 
Nothing to say
Not interested in saying anything.

A connection we try to save
We try to fake
External living, desert inside.

I wish I were interested in what you say
I wish you said more. 
A word.
I feel the bud of connection starting to bloom
But then it dies in silence

Is the lingering of the other her that causes this emptiness?
Or is she just the spotlight that makes the desert between us unbearable?

--- 

*Geometry*

Dimensions
Projections 
Models

Fitting facts to thoughts
Feelings to facts
Models to feelings.
Aiming for understanding.
A maniacal attempt to order chaos. 

The beauty of the straight line,
Its sterile artificiality. 
The safety of its controlled perfection,
the reasonable escape from you, and me, and togetherness,
and all the dangerous excitement of the unknown.
 
Surrender.
to life, 
to the absurd,
to messiness, to unpredictable 
dismissing geometry, 
its lifeless precision.

Perfection is pointless 
Against the gentle indifference of the world.
